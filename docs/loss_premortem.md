# Coronary Atlas: adversarial pre-submission review

9 October 2026. This reviews plausible ways Track A could be lost, even if the prototype works. It is a risk register, not a forecast of the judging outcome. The [official Track A statement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1T9Whog4kWF3mYB7ZMGyihGWpbJBJtsfz/view) weights prediction 30%, 3D visualization 25%, interpretability 20%, integration 15%, and implementation 10%. The [Devpost gallery](https://multimodal-ai-hackathon-2026-7.devpost.com/project-gallery) was unpublished when checked, so the rival entries below are **hypothetical**. Existing commercial/research products are adjacent prior art, not evidence that they entered this event.

## The loss story a skeptical judge might write

> Coronary Atlas is polished and unusually transparent, but its best headline result is internal evaluation of 303 selected patients. Its new-center experiment evaluates a different 13-input model, while the interactive 55-input app remains untested on independent patients. The weaker vessels miss many positive cases. The visual is reference anatomy, and no clinician has checked the source codes or vessel mapping. The team has not observed a learner using the lesson, so educational value is still a hypothesis. A competitor showed a shorter, simpler task with stronger matched validation or direct user evidence.

This is a plausible critique, not an established verdict. We should design the final demonstration to answer it explicitly. Do not claim a matched external app validation, rule-out utility, lesion localization, or measured learning improvement.

Priorities follow likely consequence and rubric weight, not invented odds. The first, third, fourth and seventh rows require new patients or people; code alone cannot close them. The 3D/integration rows can still reveal actionable UI defects without making scientific claims.

## Counterfactual rival designs worth considering

1. **Clinician-led, matched external validation.** This would be a decisive scientific advantage if the rival uses the same served predictor, compatible pre-test features, a comparable stenosis threshold and transparent exclusions. We cannot manufacture those observations from a model sweep. An external result for a different input contract is useful research but a weaker proof.
2. **A focused 5–15-input product.** A rival could have lower AUC yet win a workflow demonstration by importing data that users already have. Our 55-input contract may feel like a research form. A new smaller model should only be promoted after frozen evaluation and a user task, not by deleting fields at inference.
3. **Genuine image or waveform plus tabular model.** Raw ECG or CCTA paired with vessel labels could look more multimodal. The Track A brief explicitly permits tabular ECG/echo findings, so this is an optional competitive edge, not missing compliance. It is only an evidence advantage with patient-level separation, valid labels and accessible inference; a pasted ECG graphic would add no proof.
4. **Assessed anatomy teaching.** A rival could show that learners locate vessels or interpret model error faster than with slides. BioDigital already provides anatomy quizzes, so our switching reason must be the connection to **held-out errors and transport**, not a generic 3D tour. The case reveal now links to the reference vessel without displaying a fold probability as a live result. No benefit claim until our own users are observed.
5. **A more dramatic but wrong visual.** Patient-specific plaque animation without coordinates might wow a judge briefly. We should compete with a real mapping and make the reference/lesion boundary obvious. The official criterion values correspondence as well as interactivity.
6. **A headline score near 100%.** Such scores exist in papers on the same small source cohort, but method and endpoint must be checked before comparison. One [published example](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44248-025-00088-6) prints precision 100%, sensitivity 95.24% and F1 74.61% for the same row; the first two imply F1 about 97.56%. This arithmetic inconsistency does not prove why its AUC differs from ours. Our response is to show row-level held-out predictions, splits and denominators, while accepting that a rigorously tested rival could genuinely outperform us.
7. **Better first-minute communication.** A slick three-minute video could outrank a dense seven-minute tour if judges never discover the central task. Open on Case D's false negative, immediately name its true label and limitation, then show selection on the heart, live prediction and evidence. The [7:30 script](demo_script.md) and [4:35 judge cut](demo_script_judge_cut.md) are preparation; neither has been tested with a cold viewer or recorded.
8. **Safer public demonstration.** A rival could show a frictionless public demo while avoiding sensitive patient entry. Our app has no authentication or clinical data governance; the README instructs synthetic or de-identified inputs. If it is hosted publicly, use only synthetic examples in the judged flow and keep that instruction conspicuous. Public hosting is not listed as a Track A deliverable in the reviewed brief; a public repository and YouTube demonstration are.

## Ideas that need a hard stop condition

| Proposed addition | Continue only if | Otherwise |
|---|---|---|
| More model tuning, TabICL, oversampling or calibration | A locked task and unused comparator/test data justify replacing the served model. | Keep it as research; same-303 post-search gains do not establish winning accuracy. |
| A lower LCX/RCA cutoff | A declared error-cost objective and independent test show the sensitivity/false-alarm exchange is useful. | Keep the cutoff lab as teaching evidence, not a medical recommendation. |
| AngioCAD-derived new app model | A separately specified 13-input product and independent follow-up cohort can be built and evaluated. | Do not substitute its .781 AUC into the 55-input app. |
| 3D torso, more branches, AR or visual effects | A reviewer/user fails a specific spatial task that the addition fixes, and frame-rate/clarity remain acceptable. | Preserve the stable, explicitly reference-only 56-mesh view. |
| Patient-specific geometry or lesion markers | Paired patient imaging and lesion coordinates are available with appropriate rights and validation. | Do not infer lesion site from a whole-vessel class probability. |
| LLM clinical explanation or treatment suggestions | A clinical task, source-backed answer constraints, fidelity and error review exist. | Keep the measured SHAP explanation and educational disclaimer. |
| Clinical deployment / referral claim | Appropriate prospective evaluation, governance, clinician workflow and a justified threshold exist. | Keep the prototype educational/research only. |

## Questions to rehearse with an unsympathetic judge

| Question | Defensible one-sentence answer | Evidence to open |
|---|---|---|
| “Is .920 the accuracy of the public app on new patients?” | No: it is original-cohort internal AUC for the raw selection procedure, and the 55-input served model has no matched external test. | [Technical report](technical_report.md), [AngioCAD distinction](angiocad_external_results.md) |
| “Could I use a low LCX number to rule out disease?” | No: about 60% of LCX positives were missed by the original .5 decision and the reduced external test also missed 54/90. | [Confusion evidence](model_improvement_results.md) |
| “Why is the vessel red; where is the narrowing?” | Its color encodes a model probability for the whole label on shared reference anatomy; no lesion position or percent narrowing is inferred. | Live heart view and [anatomy provenance](../assets/README.md) |
| “Does SHAP explain the number I see?” | It exactly reconstructs the raw model output; the UI separately shows any coherence adjustment to the displayed number. | Live explanation and [coherence study](coherence_projection_results.md) |
| “What new population did you actually test?” | A separate source-trained 13-input logistic model on another selected Iranian catheterization cohort, with prespecified ambiguous endpoint exclusions. | [Frozen protocol](angiocad_external_protocol.md), [result](angiocad_external_results.md) |
| “Who needs this instead of an anatomy website?” | Medical/ML learners may need a linked lesson on model errors and transfer; that is a hypothesis pending the observed comparison. | [Research](product_research.md), [study plan](learner_validation_plan.md) |
| “Where are your actual ECG/echo images?” | The supplied Track A dataset contains ECG/echo **findings as columns**, not raw signals/images; we use those tabular findings. | [Dataset audit](dataset_analysis.md), [official brief](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1T9Whog4kWF3mYB7ZMGyihGWpbJBJtsfz/view) |
| “Can I reproduce the exact demo?” | The local build has been tested; this answer becomes yes for judges only after a public commit, cold checkout and exact-video verification. | [Verification ledger](verification.md) and future public repository |

## Before-submission decision order

1. **Now:** use the [15-minute packet](clinical_review_packet.md) with the one qualified doctor/coronary reviewer the team may be able to arrange. Record exact corrections, uncertainty and disagreement; no review has happened yet. If learners become available, use the smallest observed comparison in [learner_validation_plan.md](learner_validation_plan.md). One expert review is valuable content review, not a learner study or clinical validation.
2. **Now:** rehearse the first 90 seconds and answer the eight judge questions aloud. Fix any wording that makes .920 sound external, .781 sound like this app, a colored vessel sound like plaque, or SHAP sound causal. The app's model-info limitation now names the exact 55-input boundary.
3. **Then:** test final browser startup, 3D picking/touch/performance, one CSV import, one prediction and one export on the exact packaged build. Verify the six-page document remains legible. A previous Chromium pass is evidence of the prior build, not a future release guarantee.
4. **Release day:** create and inspect the Git commit, publish, reproduce a clean checkout, record a concise English video, inspect its final upload, verify every public link and required disclosure, then complete Devpost. The submission deadline is [15 October 2026 at 00:15 IST](https://multimodal-ai-hackathon-2026-7.devpost.com/rules); treat the previous evening as the practical cutoff.

Clinical prediction reporting guidance such as [TRIPOD+AI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38626948/) and [PROBAST+AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/388/bmj-2024-082505) reinforces the separation of model development, internal evaluation, external evaluation, applicability and subgroup uncertainty. These are useful review lenses, not extra hackathon rules. The public gallery is unavailable, so no claim of first place, a complete competitor census or a numeric win probability is supportable today.
