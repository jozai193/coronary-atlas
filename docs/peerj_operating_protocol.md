## Fixed question and design

Can local learning retain its probability-quality advantage when training and calibration are separated, and what detection/false-alarm trade-off results from cutoffs chosen without evaluation labels?

Use the unchanged 14-input mapping, all 553 rows and CHD labels from `peerj_external_protocol.md`. Retain the extreme WBC row. No feature, model-family, hyperparameter or anomaly search. The source logistic model is the already frozen 303-person model. Local logistic uses the same training-only median imputation, standardization, nominal encoding and C=.03 pipeline. No refit on all local records or deployment promotion.

Reuse the two five-fold outer partitions with seeds 20261010/20261011, for paired comparability with prior development. Within each outer training pool, permute row indices with NumPy default_rng seed `20261012 + 100*repeat_index + fold_index`. The first **111** rows are calibration; the other **331 or 332** are local model training. The outer **110 or 111** rows are evaluation. These three roles must be pairwise disjoint. Stop if any role has only one class; no rerandomization. Each of the 553 people is evaluated once per repeat; 1,106 predictions are not 1,106 independent patients.

Fixed probability arms: (1) unchanged source; (2) local-training prevalence constant; (3) source plus sigmoid on calibration rows; (4) local logistic; (5) local logistic plus sigmoid on calibration rows. Each sigmoid uses clipped logits [1e-6,1-1e-6], logistic C=1, max_iter=3000, seed=20261010. Preserve negative slopes if any. Fit the local model only on training rows; fit sigmoid only on calibration rows. Calibration rows may select cutoffs after their sigmoid fit; the separate evaluation rows assess this entire procedure. No evaluation features or labels enter either fit.

For the four nonconstant arms, save .5 and the largest observed calibration-positive score that detects at least **80%, 90%, 95%** of calibration positives. Precisely: sort positive calibration scores descending and choose index ceil(target*n_positive)-1; classify score >= cutoff, including ties. These are empirical calibration targets, not guarantees for evaluation patients. Do not choose a best target after evaluation. The **90% illustration** is the primary operating comparison, local sigmoid versus source with its own calibration-selected 90% cutoff. Also show unchanged source at .5 and local sigmoid at .5. 80% and 95% are secondary context. There is no rule-out claim or automatic pass/promotion screen.

## Reporting

Publish every arm/target and both repeats, including negative findings: Brier, log loss, average precision, mean probability, mean within-fold AUC, separately pooled OOF AUC, ten-bin reliability; confusion counts, sensitivity, specificity, precision, negative predictive value, and fraction flagged at every cutoff. Show per-repeat Wilson 95% sensitivity and specificity intervals as descriptive intervals that do not account for fitted-model uncertainty. Show per-fold realized sensitivity and number of folds below the calibration target. Publish cutoff ranges and calibration class counts.

Primary probability comparison: local sigmoid Brier minus unchanged source and local-training constant; also compare uncalibrated local logistic so sigmoid cannot receive unearned credit. Primary operating comparison: local sigmoid at calibration target .90 minus source at calibration target .90. Compute paired 1,000-draw bootstrap intervals (seed 20261012) for sensitivity and specificity differences, and Brier differences, by first averaging each person's two repeat contributions and then resampling the 553 people (class-specific subsets for sensitivity/specificity). These conditional intervals omit training, selection, center and threshold-estimation uncertainty; no hypothesis-test or clinical-success claim. All people come from previously inspected development data.

## Integrity and validation

Separate prepare, fit, evaluate and read-only verify stages, refusing overwrites. Preparation saves raw/protocol/dependency/code hashes, exact row partitions, and durable historical artifact hashes (exclude generated bytecode). Fit freezes 10 local rankers, 20 sigmoids, all thresholds, calibration predictions/labels and fit metadata before evaluation. Evaluation saves all OOF probabilities, decisions, rows and frozen results. Verify hashes, role isolation, complete row coverage, reproduced probabilities/thresholds/metrics and historical preservation. A separate verifier independently checks threshold maximality, confusion arithmetic, preprocessing and saved probability reproduction. Regression tests perturb all evaluation labels/features and assert unchanged fitted parameters/calibrators/cutoffs. Packaged saved-output verification must work without raw external workbooks; explicitly report which raw checks were skipped.
