# Prediction improvement opportunities and evidence

**Follow-up, 9 October 2026:** A fixed [InterpretML main-effects EBM trial](ebm_github_trial.md) on the same 303 patients and saved outer folds improved CAD mean AUROC from .9098 to .9285 and Brier from .1045 to .0930 against matched baseline-selector predictions. LAD AUROC was essentially unchanged; LCX and RCA worsened. This is exploratory post-search evidence, so no app model was replaced. The 55-input EBM has no independent compatible-patient validation or served explanation audit.

**Follow-up, 9 October 2026:** [AngioCAD](angiocad_external_results.md) supplied a new invasive-angiography hospital cohort with 13 defensibly mapped non-outcome inputs. A fixed, separate source-only logistic model was tested there. It is **not** a drop-in cohort for the 55-input app, and its results do not validate the deployed model. Older statements below about needing an independent compatible cohort refer to that full-input validation need.

**Follow-up, 8 October 2026:** a public CONFIRM workbook with 27,125 records was subsequently acquired and a separately authorized four-input model was tested on it. The primary CAD result was AUC .656 with substantial overprediction; the existing app remains unvalidated externally. See [the completed experiment](confirm_external_results.md). The inventory below records the earlier search and is superseded by that report for external-data availability.

This is the completed reasonable-search inventory for the current 303-row Z-Alizadeh Sani predictor. It separates finished experiments, viable follow-ups, and blocked claims. It is not a release or clinical decision. Current product scope is educational; lack of an independent cohort prevents a transportability claim but is not by itself an automatic bar to an educational prototype.

## Evidence boundary

The available model data are one small, selected cohort: 303 rows, 55 approved inputs, four angiography-derived labels, no missing cells, one Cath-versus-vessel-label inconsistency (row 93), no verified site/time/patient identifiers, and incomplete measurement definitions. Existing nested cross-validation and repeats reuse these same people. Inputs exclude all target labels, but measurement timing relative to angiography is not documented, so availability of echo and other clinical findings at the intended prediction point still needs domain review.

The existing experiments have tested a meaningful set of model families and decision/probability adjustments. The bounded TabICLv2 explanation and runtime pilot is complete; the highest-value evidence gap is still an independent compatible cohort. Further searches over this small reused cohort would add selection bias rather than trustworthy evidence.

## Candidate decisions

1. Keep the existing fitted baseline and educational cutoff panel as defaults. The completed v1 search did not establish a general raw-model improvement. The disclosed coherence layer adjusts default baseline display probabilities but does not alter fitted models or historical raw-model evaluation; the separate LCX experiment remains opt-in.
2. Keep TabICLv2 research-only: CAD clears both engineering screens; LAD and LCX clear probability quality only; RCA clears neither. The explanation pilot verifies additive reconstruction and measured local resource use, but each target/profile explanation takes about 16–19 seconds and the complete 12-case audit takes 265.8 seconds. This is too slow for an unbounded synchronous all-target interaction; an asynchronous or selected-target experiment remains technically plausible. All CV training folds are below the authors' tested row range.
3. The app exposes LCX histogram boosting as an explicit opt-in experiment while leaving the baseline as the default. That post-search experiment is reviewable without changing the meaning of promotion: its same-cohort evidence does not establish an unbiased winner or transportability. Preserve the selection caveat in the comparison surface and reserve promotion for a new locked evaluation or independent data.
4. The strongest evidence improvement is an independent, license-compatible, correctly mapped patient cohort. The 2018 authors' 500-patient extension includes the original 303, so only its added records are a possible lead; no usable public file or terms were established. Do not pool UCI 412 or substitute the incompatible datasets.

The UCI, broad-search, TabICLv2, coherence and multitask research artifacts are reviewable in `ml/experiments/{improvement_v1,broad_v2,tabicl_v2,coherence_v1,multitask_v1}/` and `data/external_audit/v2/`. The baseline model artifacts remain unchanged; its displayed probabilities may be coherence-adjusted. The separately packaged HGB LCX candidate is opt-in only; its same-cohort evidence and post-search selection caveat remain explicit.

## 9 October update: fixed EBM and a new Chinese cohort

The [55-input EBM trial](ebm_github_trial.md) found an internal CAD-only signal on reused patients. A subsequent [separate 14-input China transport experiment](peerj_external_results.md) trained fixed EBM and logistic models only on the original 303 rows and tested 553 new patients after a protocol/model freeze. EBM AUC was .664 versus .674 and Brier .333 versus .307. Both models overpredicted disease; no promotion screen passed. Sensitivity checks and subgroup tables are preserved. This does not externally validate the 55-input candidate. Source-unit/timing review, another untouched compatible cohort, and a separately frozen calibration study take priority over further searches on inspected rows.

## 10 October update: a measured adaptation direction

A [prespecified local calibration/learning development study](peerj_adaptation_results.md) now tests the overprediction hypothesis on the already inspected China cohort. The primary 64-label-per-fit sigmoid arm reduced Brier .3073 to .2121 and beat local prevalence (.2358). Larger local logistic fits improved mean within-fold AUC .6743 to .7823 and Brier to .1842. This suggests local input/label relationships matter in addition to probability scale. At .5, primary recall fell from 92.0% to 25.4%; a favorable probability-loss result cannot support a rule-out claim. Next evidence should be a reviewed intended use and untouched site/time evaluation, not a threshold chosen to repair this already viewed test. The live 55-input app remains unchanged.
