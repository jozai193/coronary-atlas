# Additional public data and useful methods - 13 October 2026

## Outcome of this pass

Subsequent fixed follow-up: [cohort-specific intercepts and separate calibration](cohort_calibration_results.md). It retained all source caveats and found only a small uncertain LCX lead; no live replacement.

Found and inspected additional public datasets, downloaded two new deposits' structured tables, reviewed primary papers, and executed a fixed multi-cohort training comparison. **No demonstrated overall accuracy gain or live model promotion.** The full app still uses its original 55-input models. The new fits use 11 shared CAD inputs / 13 vessel inputs, not fabricated values for absent ECG/echo fields.

The executed experiment trained on additional AngioCAD and Anhui patients as well as the original UCI patients: 1,201 eligible CAD records across three cohorts. These two additional cohorts were previously acquired and inspected, so the experiment is development on reused populations. The newly downloaded ICD and perioperative datasets failed the compatibility screen and were not fitted. Full numerical evidence and every arm: [multi-cohort results](multicohort_results.md).

## Papers that influenced decisions

| Primary paper | Useful method / lesson | What we did or would need |
|---|---|---|
| [Patil & Parmigiani, PNAS 2018: Training replicable predictors in multiple studies](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1708283115) | Compare study-specific learners and their combination across populations. | **Implemented:** fixed uniform study ensemble versus pooling; every cohort/arm retained. No assumption that its examples prove a coronary benefit. [Author code](https://github.com/prpatil/csml_rep) is a methods reference, not a source of new coronary patients. |
| [Guan et al.: Merging versus ensembling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.07382) | Predictor-outcome heterogeneity changes whether combining rows or combining predictors is preferable. | **Implemented:** pooled, equal study-weighted and study-ensemble comparators. Our actual result was mixed/negative, so no automatic replacement. |
| [Collins et al., BMJ 2024: Evaluation, part 1](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-074819) | Internal-external validation withholds whole centers/studies and examines heterogeneous performance. | **Implemented:** whole-cohort holdouts plus separate within-cohort augmentation; Brier, AUC, reliability, confusion counts and every destination reported. Reused cohorts remain development evidence. |
| [TRIPOD-Cluster explanation](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2022-071058) | Explicit cluster definitions and per-cluster performance help expose weaknesses hidden by pooled summaries. | **Implemented:** fixed study membership, grouped identical inputs, saved train/test roles and equal-cohort summaries alongside per-cohort results. This is not a claim of full TRIPOD compliance. |
| [Song et al., BMC 2026: multi-vessel disease](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-026-03437-w) | Their validation favored the simpler logistic model's robustness over more complex alternatives; they also examined calibration. | Retain a simple comparator; do not assume complexity gives higher accuracy. Their model's intraprocedural endpoint and angiographic/treatment inputs are unsuitable shortcuts for our pre-test feature contract. TyG is a possible prespecified derived-feature ablation, not an accepted accuracy improvement. |
| [McElfresh et al.: When do neural nets outperform boosted trees?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02997) | Performance depends on the dataset; broad tabular benchmarks do not identify a universal winning architecture. | Our existing tree/logistic/CatBoost/TabICL research remains relevant. Another large architecture search on the same 303 patients would require new evidence to avoid selecting a flattering score. |
| [Zheng et al., MALAT1 study](https://doi.org/10.1080/07853890.2026.2663194) | Investigates an additional biomarker alongside clinical variables; echo function and angiographic CAD are separate observations. | New biomarker or echo variables require actual matching measurements and a new validated input contract. Associations do not support forcing CAD probabilities to equal EF or valve findings. |

## Concrete conclusion

The literature supplied a testable training strategy, and we executed it rather than assuming that more data helps. CAD augmentation worsened probability quality; the small LCX improvement is uncertain and reduced original-cohort detection at .5. Keep the live app unchanged. The most valuable next evidence is a genuinely compatible new clinical cohort or clarified field definitions and an intended-use/error-cost objective from the reviewer. Any further TyG, nonlinear, calibration or imbalance trial must be a separate fixed comparison, with all results retained and no held-out-data tuning.
